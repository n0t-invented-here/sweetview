How the icon was clicked. The item asks for a click by hand. No person is at this machine, so the click was
made with real mouse input instead: rust-rewrite/checks/tray-real-click.ps1 finds the icon Explorer shows (in
the taskbar's overflow flyout here) through UI Automation by its tooltip, moves the cursor onto it and injects
the button press with SendInput. From there on it is what a hand does: Explorer takes the click and posts the
icon's callback message to the app, the app builds and opens its menu, and the keys typed choose an item.
rust-rewrite/checks/toast-real-click.ps1 does the same for the toast banner. A person clicking is left to the
owner (rust-rewrite/OWNER-ACTIONS.md, section 4).
